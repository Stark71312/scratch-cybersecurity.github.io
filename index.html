// DEFINITION

Understanding Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, servers, mobile devices, electronic systems, networks, and data from digital attacks, theft, damage, and unauthorized access.

In an era where virtually everything is connected, cybersecurity forms the digital immune system of our society — guarding sensitive data, critical infrastructure, personal privacy, and national security.

It encompasses technologies, processes, and practices designed to defend against the ever-evolving landscape of cyber threats — from lone hackers to nation-state actors.

// THREAT LANDSCAPE

Known Threats & Attacks

🦠

Malware

Malicious software — viruses, trojans, ransomware — designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to systems.

🎣

Phishing

Fraudulent communications that appear to come from a reputable source, tricking users into revealing sensitive information.

👤

Man-in-the-Middle

Attackers intercept communication between two parties to eavesdrop or alter data in transit without detection.

💥

DDoS Attack

Overwhelming a server or network with traffic from multiple sources to disrupt normal service availability.

// CORE PRINCIPLES

The CIA Triad

C
Confidentiality

Only authorized individuals can access sensitive information. Enforced through encryption, access controls, and authentication protocols.

I
Integrity

Data must remain accurate and trustworthy. Checksums, digital signatures, and version control ensure information hasn't been tampered with.

A
Availability

Systems and data must be accessible when needed. Achieved through redundancy, failover systems, and protection against denial-of-service attacks.

// DEFENSE PROTOCOL

How to Stay Protected

01
🔑

Strong Passwords

Use unique, complex passwords and a password manager. Enable multi-factor authentication everywhere possible.

02
🔄

Regular Updates

Keep operating systems and software patched. Most breaches exploit known vulnerabilities with available fixes.

03
💾

Backup Data

Follow the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, 2 different media types, 1 offsite. Essential for ransomware recovery.

04
🎓

Security Awareness

Train employees and yourself to recognize phishing, social engineering, and suspicious activity patterns.

05
🛡️

Zero Trust Model

Never trust, always verify. Grant least-privilege access and treat every request as potentially compromised.

06
🔐

Encrypt Everything

Encrypt data at rest and in transit. Use HTTPS, VPNs on public networks, and encrypted storage solutions.

07
👁️

Monitor & Detect

Implement SIEM tools and continuous monitoring. The average breach goes undetected for over 200 days.

08
📋

Incident Response

Prepare a response plan before an attack happens. Know your containment, eradication, and recovery procedures.
